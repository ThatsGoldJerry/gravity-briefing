F100 Language Analysis

AI Investment Talk Is Outrunning Governance 6 to 1

Corporate leaders are leaning heavily into capability narratives, but public filings reveal a widening gap between what companies promise and the concrete governance structures in place to back it up.

The Dataset: Gravity Research analyzed 2,696 Fortune 100 public documents—including earnings calls, 10-K/8-K filings, and issue reports published from January 2025 through April 2026.

Key Benchmark Findings

⚡ Agentic AI surges +71%, outpacing governance talk 6:1
Mentions of autonomous agent deployments nearly doubled YoY. Yet, explicit language around oversight—such as review boards, committees, and human-in-the-loop controls—barely moved, leaving organizations exposed when agent actions encounter scrutiny.
📈 Capex rises +30% as ROI skepticism spikes +121%
Capex talk continues to outrun revenue mentions (+20%). Terms referencing model inaccuracy, adverse effects, and delayed payback climbed sharply, elevating the hurdle for proof across boardrooms and analyst calls.
👥 Layoffs undercut the AI augmentation narrative as climate talk drops -18%
Firms mention workforce augmentation 4.5x more often than workforce reductions (+37% vs +19%), carrying reputational risks as headcount cuts persist. Concurrently, data center energy and emissions disclosures declined 18%.
